FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need to enter a credit card to start?
Yes, but you won't be charged a thing until day 15. If you cancel before then, you pay nothing. We ask for it upfront so there's no interruption when your trial converts.
Can I import my existing spreadsheets?
Yes. Every module has a CSV template download and import flow. If you're already tracking this stuff in Excel or Google Sheets, you can be up and running in under 10 minutes.
How does the ordering send work?
You set a preferred send method for each distributor — email, SMS, or online portal note. When you submit an order, it goes to each rep their preferred way automatically.
What if my distributor responds to the order?
Rep replies route back into the app as notifications. You'll see a badge when a rep responds so you're not hunting through your texts or inbox.
How does pricing work?
Front of House is $15/month per location — that covers ordering, pour cost, and COGS. Back of House (kitchen tools) is a $10/month add-on. Bundle both for $25/month. Managing multiple locations? Each gets its own account at the same price.
When is the Inventory module coming?
It's in active development and will be included in the same $15/month — no price change, no upgrade required. Subscribers get it automatically when it launches.
